01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SPREADSHEETS

Hi, I’m Haley. I ask a lot of questions.

I'm a research-minded professional with a background in criminal justice and applied statistics. I earned my M.S. in Applied Statistics & Research Methods from the University of Northern Colorado and my B.A. in Sociology, with a concentration in Criminology, from Colorado State University.

I enjoy the part of a project where something complicated starts to make sense—whether that's making sense of research findings, identifying discrepancies in data, or designing a more useful workflow.
